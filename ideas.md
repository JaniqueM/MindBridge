# MindBridge visual direction — A gentle mind, a caring heart

## Design direction

Follow the user's revised direction: a polished, warm, welcoming wellbeing website for people aged **13 and older**, with a more blended colour atmosphere and a distinct heart-and-mind identity. Keep the bridge-to-human-support idea in the content, but replace the bridge-and-sun logo. The interface should feel supportive and credible—not a hospital portal, crisis-monitoring system, or generic corporate app.

- **Design movement:** Soft editorial minimalism with calm, human geometry and a caring, contemporary finish.
- **Core principles:** Safety before spectacle; warmth without sentimentality; clear consent; age-aware privacy; direct paths to people; accessible by default; quiet confidence in high-stress moments.
- **Color philosophy:** Keep deep plum/indigo for trust and legibility. Blend muted coral/rose, lavender and a touch of soft mint/teal through low-contrast background gradients, card tints and layered accents. Use warm cream as breathing space and soft gold only for highlights. Avoid harsh colour blocking, alarmist red outside true crisis actions, and high-saturation gradients.
- **Layout paradigm:** Spacious desktop shell with clear separate Journal and Mood navigation; mobile bottom navigation with Journal immediately discoverable. Keep the journal form and saved reflections straightforward to find. Respect safe-area insets and keep emergency access visible.
- **Signature elements:** A simple heart and mind/brain mark, clear negative space, softly blended colour fields, rounded but restrained cards, and friendly progress cues.
- **Interaction philosophy:** One clear primary action per step, gentle confirmation, clear validation, simple back/help/settings/logout, an obvious Journal path from both navigation and Home, no hidden critical routes, and no manipulative streak pressure.
- **Animation:** Very subtle background motion and short transitions; honor `prefers-reduced-motion` and ensure motion is never needed to perceive state.
- **Typography system:** Bricolage Grotesque (or similar humanist display face) for headings, paired with Figtree (or Inter fallback) for body copy. Maintain readable size, line height, and visible focus.
- **Brand essence:** MindBridge connects thoughtful self-care with a caring heart and a next step toward human support.
- **Brand voice:** Calm, respectful, direct, non-judgmental, age-inclusive for people 13 and older. Avoid diagnosis, promises, or language that implies crisis monitoring. Use natural brand-forward copy; avoid repetitive “demo” labels while clearly explaining prototype limitations in the privacy/help content.
- **Wordmark/logo:** One simple heart-and-mind symbol with a confident, recognisable silhouette. Reuse the same concept in the header, favicon and project icon; keep the full-bleed project icon distinct from the hero artwork.
- **Signature brand colors:** Muted coral `#E98988`, deep plum-violet `#5543A8`, pale lavender `#F3EEF9`, with soft mint `#DDF2EC` and warm cream `#FBF6F2` as blended supporting colours.

## Asset placement

Use the existing wide, text-free night-to-dawn hero illustration in the landing panel; keep the left side quiet for editable heading and CTA copy. Replace the project icon/header/favicons with the new heart-and-mind artwork, matching the updated palette and retaining high recognition at small sizes. The site continues to disclose that its account, booking, referral, and chat features are a student-built prototype rather than a live clinical service.
